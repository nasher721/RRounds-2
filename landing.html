Everything in one workspace

Built for the rhythm of rounding.

Document, task, and hand off without switching tools — each section stays close to the chart it belongs to.

10-system review

Neuro, CV, respiratory, renal, GI, endocrine, heme, infectious, skin/lines, and disposition — one consistent structure per patient.

Medication management

Infusions, scheduled meds, and PRNs tracked separately, with dose calculators and interaction checks on hand.

Clinical phrases

Reusable text blocks shared across your team. Type a shortcut and expand to a full, consistent phrase.

AI with guardrails

Draft interval events, summarize changes, and format medications — every suggestion waits for your review before it lands in the chart.

Export & sign-off

PDF, Excel, and print-ready rounds documents. One-click sign-off confirms completeness across every section.

Offline support

Keep charting when the network drops. Edits queue locally and sync when you're back online.
